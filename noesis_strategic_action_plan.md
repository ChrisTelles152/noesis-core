## Risk Mitigation Strategies

### Technical Risks
**Algorithm Performance Risk**: FSRS may not optimize effectively for Brazilian learning patterns
- *Mitigation*: Extensive A/B testing with fallback to proven BKT-only approaches
- *Early Warning*: Monitor retention prediction accuracy vs baseline metrics

**Attention Tracking Accuracy**: WebGazer performance in varied conditions
- *Mitigation*: Hybrid explicit/implicit attention detection with graceful degradation  
- *Early Warning*: User complaints about calibration or false attention detection

### Market Risks  
**Economic Conditions**: Brazilian economic volatility affecting EdTech spending
- *Mitigation*: Flexible pricing tiers and institutional partnership development
- *Early Warning*: Monitor broader EdTech sector funding and customer acquisition trends

**Competitive Response**: International platforms entering Brazilian market faster than anticipated
- *Mitigation*: Accelerated market entry with strong local partnerships
- *Early Warning*: Job postings, partnership announcements, localized content development

### Execution Risks
**User Adoption**: Elite students preferring established international platforms  
- *Mitigation*: Superior efficacy demonstration through rigorous research validation
- *Early Warning*: Low pilot program completion rates or poor Net Promoter Scores

**Content Quality**: AI-generated content failing Brazilian educational standards
- *Mitigation*: Enhanced expert review process and automated quality assurance
- *Early Warning*: Expert reviewer rejection rates >30% or user complaints about content accuracy
